Delete your Space Xplorer account

Space Xplorer (Android, Google Play) – developer "solodev92" · Last updated: 22 October 2026

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Space Xplorer and go to Settings.
  2. Open Account & Cloud and, under Danger Zone, tap DELETE ACCOUNT & DATA.
  3. Read what will be deleted, tick I understand and tap CONTINUE.
  4. Type DELETE, then tap DELETE FOREVER.

This needs an internet connection. The game restarts as a new player when it is done.

Without the game

Email nassif.bs@gmail.com with the subject "Space Xplorer account deletion" and your Player ID (shown in the game under Profile, with a COPY button). If you no longer have the game, tell us the username you used. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your Unity player account and its Google Play Games link (your Google account itself is not affected).
  • Your username, which becomes available to others.
  • Your entries on the global leaderboards.
  • Your cloud save, Captain's Inbox read and claim records, and leaderboard career and weekly challenge records.
  • Analytics data linked to your device (if you allowed analytics).
  • When deleting in the game: all of the game's data on that device except sound and control settings.

What is kept

  • A record that a deletion was requested (the Player ID and the date), to show the request was honoured.
  • For a limited time, the backend service logs, which may contain the Player ID.
  • Results of past weekly challenges stay in the archived standings as a score without any name.

The game has no paid subscriptions, so there is nothing to cancel first. See also the privacy policy.
