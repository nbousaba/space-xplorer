Space Xplorer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Space Xplorer ("the game", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by an independent developer (Google Play developer account "solodev92").

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (missions, Explore progress, ships, upgrades, Dilithium, Titanium, achievements, Captain's Records and settings) is saved on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes this copy.

2. Online player account (Unity Gaming Services)

When you are online, the game creates an anonymous player account with Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies), identified by a random Player ID. It is used to deliver Captain's Inbox messages and gifts, to remember which messages you have read and which gifts you have claimed, and to keep the online features secure. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Unity processes this data on our behalf: Unity Privacy Policy.

3. Google Play Games and cloud save (optional)

If you choose Link Google Play Games, the game signs you in with Google Play Games and links your Google Play Games player identity to your Space Xplorer Player ID, so you can sign in to the same profile on another device. We receive your Google Play Games player ID; we do not receive your Google password or email address.

Linked players' game progress (the items listed in section 1) is backed up to Unity Cloud Save under their Player ID, so it can be restored on another device. Guests are not backed up. Linking is optional and the game can be played without it. Google Privacy Policy.

4. Usernames (optional)

Players linked to Google Play Games can choose a public username. It is stored by our game server with your Player ID and will be shown to other players, for example on leaderboards. Do not use your real name or other personal information as a username. You can change it at any time in the game; an old username is released for other players when you change it. We keep a short history of recent username changes to resolve disputes or abuse.

5. Gameplay statistics (optional)

With your permission (asked once in the game, and changeable any time in Profile > Captain's Records), the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Unity Analytics: for example which levels were played, completed or failed, how long missions and Explore trips took, which power-ups were chosen and how Dilithium and Titanium were earned and spent. These are linked to a random analytics identifier, not to your name or contact details, and we do not collect precise location. If you decline, nothing is sent and the game works the same.

Unity Analytics also records basic session and device information (such as app version, device model and country derived from your IP address) as part of its service: Unity Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Google settings. How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

7. Purchases

Any in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Online account data (Player ID, Inbox read and claim records, cloud save, username, leaderboard scores) is kept while your account is in use.

Delete your account in the game: Settings > Account & Cloud > DELETE ACCOUNT & DATA. After you confirm (tick "I understand" and type DELETE) this deletes your username, your leaderboard entries, your cloud save and Inbox records, asks Unity Analytics to delete the data linked to your device, deletes your Unity player account (which also removes its Google Play Games link - your Google account itself is not affected) and clears the game's data on that device. It needs an internet connection; if it is interrupted, the game shows it as pending, saves nothing to the account in the meantime, and finishes it the next time you play online. A leaderboard entry that cannot be removed at once is removed by us shortly afterwards and shows no name until then.

Without the game: see Delete your Space Xplorer account - email us your Player ID and we will delete the same data within 30 days.

What we keep: a record that a deletion was requested (the Player ID and the date), to show the request was honoured, and, for a limited time, the backend service logs, which may contain the Player ID. Results of past weekly challenges stay in the archived standings as a score without any name.
